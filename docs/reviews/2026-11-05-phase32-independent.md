## Taste calls the owner should decide (reviewer's list, unchanged by me)

- Plate colour: warm plate + mint thread = two accent hues on one win. A mono-gold thread is an alternative.
- Mint thread is ~2.4px at 1280 and shows only between symbols.
- No dimming of non-winning symbols (would speed comprehension, costs the "no obscuring" balance).
- `← Lounge` is borderless, reads as a label, and nearly vanishes while disabled during a spin.
- Marcellus is inscriptional/Trajan-like, closer to classical than to "lamplit private club"; reviewer agrees with the choice on legibility. Nameplate contrast is adequate (≥ 4.9:1), not generous.
- Pre-existing, not introduced here: lounge eyebrows and gold tracked HUD labels (craft-floor "kicker" refusal), ◆ and ▶ glyph icons.

## The pop-over covering the lower reel row

The reviewer says this should be fixed rather than recorded, because previews are core to the brief. It is visible in the preview sheets and covers about half the bottom row for the lines on it. I did not fix it: the options (open it into the free area beside the payout figure, or show the previewed line's summary inside the HUD) change the Phase 3.1 win-details layout and need an owner choice. Accessibility of the preview through pointer and keyboard is unaffected; what is affected is how much of the lower row stays visible while previewing.
