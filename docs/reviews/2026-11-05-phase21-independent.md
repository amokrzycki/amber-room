# Amber Room Phase 2.1 — Independent critical design review

## Verdict

**PASS with two subjective reservations.** The revision removes the most distracting cabinet ornament, reduces the frame to a clear outer joinery edge and one reel bezel, introduces a coherent serif/sans pairing, and gives the main action a distinct physical treatment. The settled wallet remains legible and the cabinet still belongs to the burgundy and warm-brass lounge. This review does not rely on Phase 2's earlier visual approval.

## Reservations

1. **The nameplate looks applied rather than engraved.** Its restrained size and position are good; its type is flat against the brass. A small inset or low-contrast letterpress treatment could improve that physical reading, provided it does not reintroduce layered framing.
2. **The cash-out guidance is small at the top-right.** It is legible at the reviewed sizes, but it competes with the nameplate's horizontal band. The action itself is clear and the note can be read without entering a dialog; keep an eye on it in the pending 1600/1920 captures before changing the layout.

## Scope and limits

This was an independent design/source review with inspection of the final four-size capture set and its checks. I did not execute the test suite, listen to audio, use an actual screen reader, or test Safari/Firefox or real hardware. The captures show CSS viewport appearance in software-rendered Chromium, not GPU/device performance. Mixed cash-out/spin same-frame input is covered by the implementation guard; its dedicated focused rerun remained in progress when this review was updated.
