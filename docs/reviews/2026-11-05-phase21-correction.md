## Accounting and exit lifecycle

`requestSpin` is the sole spin-settlement path. It atomically creates the record and calculates:

`new balance = old balance − recorded total stake + gross payout`

`owner.commit(result.state)` saves balance, outcome and next spin ID together before reel playback. The spinning and celebrating phases are presentation locks, not unpaid winnings. There is no pending pot and no new COLLECT credit path.

Cash out refuses during moving reels. Once they stop, it can immediately skip a celebration, finish outstanding display counters and return to the lounge. It does not mutate the save or credit tokens. A local leaving guard makes repeat exits harmless and also prevents SPIN from placing a bet after navigation has been queued. Starting another spin after a result uses the already-settled wallet. The first press during a celebration remains SKIP, preserving the existing deliberate-spin behavior.

Reload reconstructs a ready session from the already-settled version-1 save. It shows the saved result statically, without replaying settlement, counting or a new result announcement. The key `fruit-salon:v1`, save version, machine math, symbols and paylines remain unchanged. Existing storage warnings and damaged-save protection remain intact.

## Animation and accessible outcomes

`NumberDisplay` owns only visible integers. It never writes game state. Changes interpolate for 320ms using cubic ease-out, then render the exact authoritative target. A new target starts from the current displayed integer and cancels the old frame. Duplicate refresh does not restart motion. New spins finish previous counters; skip, hidden-page and exit paths finish or cancel their work; scene shutdown disposes callbacks.

The balance display waits until reel completion so it does not reveal the saved return while reels are moving. Gross payout and net animate together at completion. The visible return/stake breakdown retains the recorded stake; the net field reserves its final width. Tabular lining figures and fixed HUD/control geometry avoid digit-width layout jitter. Cash out has no transfer animation because it transfers nothing; the lounge immediately shows the exact saved wallet.

Animated text is hidden from assistive technology. A separate polite, atomic result region receives the full final stake/return/net/balance statement once per new spin. A static accessible saved-result summary remains available on reload. Both the OS `prefers-reduced-motion` preference and the in-game setting bypass numeric interpolation; the OS preference also feeds existing reduced win effects. Reel timing is unchanged.
